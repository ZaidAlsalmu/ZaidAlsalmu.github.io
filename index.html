Featured Project

A course project I am proud of — built as part of CS3081 at Effat University.

CS3081  ·  AI  ·  Spring 2026

Hajj Traffic Management
System (HTMS)

A four-agent multi-agent simulation that manages extreme vehicular traffic surges during the Hajj pilgrimage. The system combines Bayesian congestion estimation, CSP-based signal control, BDI vehicle navigation, and rule-based emergency pre-emption — all coordinated through a FIPA-ACL message bus.

Key contribution: RAPCSP (Ritual-Aware Predictive CSP) — a novel extension that uses the known Hajj schedule to pre-allocate green time before surges arrive at the sensor.
